# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Starfall Idle is a welcoming place to build and discuss a science-fiction idle RPG. Everyone who participates in this project is expected to help make the community respectful, constructive, and safe, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or perspective.

## Expected behaviour

Please:

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing ideas and changes.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person who made it.
- Welcome newcomers and explain decisions clearly.
- Respect different play styles, accessibility needs, and creative viewpoints.
- Help keep issues, pull requests, and discussions focused and useful.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following are not welcome in project spaces:

- Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery in public project spaces.
- Trolling, repeated bad-faith disruption, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Abusive, insulting, or exclusionary comments.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that do not follow this Code of Conduct. Repeated or serious violations may lead to removal from project spaces.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the project maintainer through GitHub. Please include enough context for the report to be understood, such as links, screenshots, and dates when available. Reports will be handled as discreetly as possible.
